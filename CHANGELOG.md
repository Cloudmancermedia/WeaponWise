# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for the WoW Forever client (interface 16001).

- Weapon tooltips show your skill with weapons you know, for example
  "You know this weapon skill (50/60)".
- Weapons your class can learn show the weapon master, city, district,
  coordinates, cost and any level requirement. The nearest trainer is listed
  first.
- Hold Shift to list other trainers for the weapon. The tooltip updates while
  you hover.
- Ctrl + Right-Click a weapon in your bags, on your character or at a merchant
  to pin its trainer on the map. Uses TomTom instead if you set `preferTomTom`.
- Weapons Forever added to a class are tagged "New in Forever": two-handed axes
  and maces for Shamans, one-handed axes for Rogues, polearms for Druids.
- Weapons your class can't use say so in red.
- Settings through `/ww config` and `/ww set`.
